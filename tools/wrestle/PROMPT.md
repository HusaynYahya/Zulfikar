## Style, camera and people (every run)

A single continuous shot in black-and-white line art, drawn exactly like the
first frame: clean, even, pure-black ink outlines on a plain pure-white
background. Only outlines: everything inside and outside them stays pure white,
with no shading, grey, colour or shadow, and no floor line or background. The
movement looks rotoscoped from real footage of two wrestlers: realistic adult

The camera is locked off: an exact side-on profile view whose framing never
changes, with no zoom, pan or cut.

Two young men at wrestling practice. The man on the left wears a short-sleeved
t-shirt and shorts and faces right; the man on the right wears a sleeveless vest
and shorts and faces left. Their bodies, clothes and faces stay the same
throughout, and each keeps to his own side.

## Action — full run (k00 → k09, 10 s)

They start apart in low wrestling stances: knees bent, hips back, hands up. They
close the distance with short shuffle steps, taking turns, so one steps while
the other holds his ground. Each foot lifts, travels forward and plants; a
planted foot stays exactly where it is. They stay low and balanced, with a
slight bob on each step. At arm's length the man on the left reaches for the
other man's head, then the man on the right reaches back and their hands meet.
They hand-fight, pushing at each other's wrists. Then each cups the back of the
other's neck with one hand and grips the other's elbow with the other, and they
pull in until their foreheads press together, locked in a collar-and-elbow tie,
braced and leaning into each other.

The motion is smooth and continuous from start to finish.
